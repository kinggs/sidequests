| R52 | **How the family changed** (primary, each model's guide). PO-14 and PO-16: the PO-12's words. PO-20: "hold FX and press any key 1-16 to toggle effects ... some effects are mutually exclusive. fill-in, retrigger and glitch effects last until the next pattern boundary". PO-24 and PO-28: some effects also work stopped; "press FX briefly to deactivate". PO-32: "to record effects, first enter rec mode"; cleared by fx alone in rec mode. PO-33 and PO-35: "if write mode is enabled, the effects will be saved in the pattern"; cleared with write on by fx + key 16.
