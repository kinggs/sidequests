Key states

Every key carries two lights. The field is the whole face of the key. The frame is a band around its edge. Field means "there is something here". Frame means "the playhead is here". White means "this key is sounding now": pulled in from the edge under your finger, full size when the sequencer fires it.

GRID KEY
offBoth lights out. The numeral stays readable.
stepField lit, steady. The numeral turns dark.
playingFrame lit. Field stays dark.
step + playingField and frame, with a dark line between them.
pressedField goes white and pulls in from the edge.
GRID KEY · MORE
other soundWrite on. One stitch in the corner: another sound has a step here.
step + otherWrite on. The stitch turns dark on a lit field.
chosenA bar along the bottom: the sound or pattern now selected.
firedWrite off. The whole face flashes white as this key's sound plays.
queuedBroken frame: this pattern plays next. For chaining, later.
