Sounds

Each key plays the sound the engine builds in code, or a sample from the starter pack. A lit switch plays its sample. Tap a name to hear it; tap its switch to change it and hear the change. through fx is lit while the sound goes through the effects; tap it to keep the sound clean. The beat, these choices and the sliders stay in this browser.

Playing it

Hold sound and tap a key to choose a sound. write turns the keys into the chosen sound's sixteen steps. play starts and stops. Hold bpm: a key sets the volume, slider A the swing, slider B the tempo; tap it alone for 80, 120 and 140. Hold function and press pattern to clear. With a keyboard: 1234 qwer asdf zxcv are the keys, 7 8 9 sound, pattern and bpm, u i o p function, fx, play and write. The sliders are your hand: they set every sound, and a step keeps where they were when it was written. The thin line on a slider is what the chosen sound was last played with. Double-click (or double-tap) a slider to put it back to the middle; with bpm held, to swing 0 and 120 bpm. While it plays, hold fx and a key for an effect: it is heard at once, the display says fx and its number, and the steps it plays over keep it (a small mark above each). Hold fx alone to take the effects off the steps it passes. With a mouse: Ctrl+click (Cmd+click on a Mac) sound, pattern, bpm or function to hold it down; the next click on a key lets it go (so Ctrl+click sound, click 16), or Ctrl+click it again. Ctrl+click fx stays on until you Ctrl+click it again: each click on a key then plays that effect for as long as you hold it.
