Sounds

Each key plays the sound the engine builds in code, or a sample from the starter pack. A lit button plays its sample. The beat, these choices and the sliders stay in this browser.

Playing it

Hold sound and tap a key to choose a sound. write turns the keys into the chosen sound's sixteen steps. play starts and stops. Hold bpm: a key sets the volume, slider A the swing, slider B the tempo; tap it alone for 80, 120 and 140. Hold function and press pattern to clear. With a keyboard: 1234 qwer asdf zxcv are the keys, 7 8 9 sound, pattern and bpm, u i o p function, fx, play and write.
