How to play
  1. 13 dice in the cup. Green are easy victims (3 brains), yellow are even, red fight back (3 shotguns).
  2. Roll takes three dice from the cup and rolls them.
  3. Brain: eaten — set aside and counted. Shot: set aside. Ran (feet): they got away; those dice get rolled again.
  4. Roll again (your runners plus new dice, back up to three), or Stop to bank this turn's brains.
  5. Three shotguns in one turn and you're shot — you lose every brain from that turn.
  6. First to 13 brains finishes the round so everyone has the same turns. Most brains wins; a tie plays off.
  7. If the cup runs out, your eaten brain dice go back in to be rolled again. You keep every brain you've counted.

Hunk & Hottie

  1. Two yellow dice come out; the Hunk (black, white ring) and the Hottie (black, pink ring) go in.
  2. The Hunk fights: 2 feet, 2 shotguns, a double shotgun and a double brain. The Hottie runs: 3 feet, 2 shotguns, 1 brain.
  3. They save each other. If one of them shoots you while the other is in your brain pile (or eaten on the same roll), the other is rescued: back in the cup, and you lose those brains.
