**Subject:** Your Inggs family tree — a revival, with your blessing

Kenny Inggs here, [how you're related, in a few words].

I've been thinking about the family tree you researched and put up at familytree.inggs.com,

1. Your blessing. It's your research, and I'd like it to stay yours. Every page will say

2. The original data. The site was built from a GEDCOM file in July 2006. If you still have that,

   can rebuild from archived pages. The photographs too, if you have them at better than web
   size.

3. Would the recovered tree be useful to you? Most of the 2,332 aren't on WikiTree yet. I'm happy
   to send you the whole thing as a GEDCOM, if that helps you add them there.

If you'd like to be part of the family app, with full rights to edit and correct, I'd be glad.
If you'd rather I didn't do this at all, tell me and I'll stop.

Either way, thank you for doing the work in the first place. I'd have had no idea where we came
from without it.

Warm regards,
Kenny
