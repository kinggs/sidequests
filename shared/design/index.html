Connect and shared games

One gesture: a phone shows a QR, a camera opens the app. Three codes behind it (My QR, Game QR, League QR), one screen. Many phones, one game: every phone is a full mirror. At a table anyone taps anything and an echo strip says what the other phone just did, with Undo. In turn games only the current player's phone (and the host's) is live. Someone scoring on others' behalf sees a banner; a rated result then needs both players to confirm. Full guidance in proposed/shared/CONNECT.md.
